## What drives the cost of a bathroom renovation

Bathrooms vary widely by specification and size. Four factors shape the price most.

**Room size and layout.** A larger room needs more tiling and more waterproofing. Moving the toilet or the drain changes the plumbing and adds labour.

**Structural work.** Removing a partition wall adds work before fitting can start. Our logistics team handles structural removal in the pre-site phase, which usually lasts one to two weeks, so the room is clear before installation begins.

**Specification.** Your choice of tiles and fixtures sets much of the material cost. We work with high-quality brands, and the finishes are to your taste.

**Access and time on site.** A bathroom usually takes two to six weeks on site, depending on size, scope, layout changes and access. Time on site is part of the labour cost, so a difficult entrance or limited parking can add to the total.

## Where to save and where not to

Save where your choices are open. Pick tiles and taps early, so your budget is set before the design stage begins. If you buy your own fixtures through the fit only route, you control that part of the spend directly. Keeping the existing layout also helps, because moving pipework adds labour.

Do not save on the work behind the walls. Our bathroom stages run in a set order: strip back to brick, first fix plumbing, tiling, then second fix. Each stage depends on the one before it, so a change made late usually costs more to put right than one agreed at the start.

Check whether specialist oversight is included in any price you compare. Some competitors subcontract work without it, so two quotes may not cover the same level of care. Our quotes include specialist oversight.

## Getting an accurate quote

An accurate quote depends on accurate information. Before you ask for one, work through these steps.

1. Measure the room and note where the door, window, drain and radiators sit.
2. List anything you want to move, such as the toilet or the shower.
3. Decide whether you want fit only or full design and build.
4. If you are buying fixtures yourself, list each item with its model and size before the visit.

When the quote arrives, check the labour breakdown and confirm that waste removal and snagging are included. Ask whether specialist oversight is covered. Then ask about the timetable: the pre-site phase usually takes one to two weeks, and the bathroom itself usually takes two to six weeks on site.
