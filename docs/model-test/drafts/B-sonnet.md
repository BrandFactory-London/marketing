## What drives bathroom renovation cost in Essex

Five things move the price most.

**Size.** A bigger room means more tiling, more materials and more labour hours.

**Specification.** We work with high-quality brands, and the materials are to your taste. A premium tile or a freestanding bath costs more than a standard one. That choice is yours.

**Layout changes.** Keeping the toilet, basin and shower where they are is the cheapest route. Moving them means new pipework. Removing a partition wall adds structural work. Both lengthen the job.

**Time on site.** Bathrooms take two to six weeks. The range depends on size, scope, whether the layout changes and access. Longer jobs mean more labour in the quote.

**How you work with us.** Most customers come to us fit only. You have already bought the bathroom or fixtures from a showroom, and we install them and renovate the room around them. The alternative is full design, supply and build, where we run a consultation, visit a showroom with you, specify the bathroom and produce a final drawing before we fit it. The second route includes a design stage, so the total differs.

## Where to save and where not to

**Save by fixing your plan before work starts.** The main causes of delay are changes to plans, delivery errors, mixed-up drawings and access issues. Each one adds days, and days add cost. Choose your fixtures and tiles early. Agree the drawing before the strip-out begins.

**Save by checking products before you buy.** If you have bought something that will not work in the space, our specialist team will talk it through with you and arrange a replacement or refund. Better still, show us the product before you pay for it.

**Do not save on the sequence of work.** A bathroom goes through four stages in order: strip back to brick, first fix plumbing, tiling, then second fix. Rushing or skipping a stage hides problems behind finished tiles.

**Do not save on oversight.** Our quote includes specialist oversight. Some competitors subcontract the work without it, and that is where mistakes slip through.

**Do not strip waste removal or snagging out of a quote.** Both are included in ours. A cheaper quote that leaves them out only moves the cost to you later.

## Getting an accurate quote

You can get close to a real figure with a little preparation.

1. Decide whether you want fit only or full design, supply and build.
2. If you have already bought a bathroom or fixtures, have the details ready.
3. Note whether you want to move any plumbing or remove a wall.
4. Tell us about access, including parking and stairs.
