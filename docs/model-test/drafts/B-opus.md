## Typical bathroom renovation cost in Essex

Bathrooms vary more in price than almost any other room, because specification and size swing so widely. A small en suite with a like-for-like layout sits at the lower end of [Migss bathroom price range]. A large family bathroom with a new layout and premium fixtures sits at the top.

## What drives the cost

### Size and specification

A bigger room needs more tiles, more labour hours and often more pipework. Specification matters just as much. Materials are chosen to your taste, and we work with high-quality brands, so the spread between a standard and a premium finish can be large.

### Changing the layout

Keeping the toilet, basin and shower where they are is the cheapest option. Moving a toilet usually means rerouting the waste pipe to the soil stack, which adds plumbing time and may mean lifting floors. Any layout change also affects time on site. Our bathrooms take two to six weeks on site, and a new layout pushes a project towards the longer end.

### Structural work and access

Some projects need a partition wall removed. Our logistics team handles this in the pre-site phase, which usually lasts one to two weeks, along with scheduling deliveries and clearing existing furniture and strip-out waste. Access also counts. A top-floor flat with a narrow stairwell takes longer to strip and supply than a ground-floor room.

## Where to save and where not to

You can save money by settling your choices before work starts. Changes to plans are one of the main causes of delay, and delays cost labour time. If you already know the fixtures you want, the fit only route keeps design costs off your bill.

Check that a product suits the room before you buy it. A freestanding bath that blocks the door is money wasted. If you have already bought something that will not work in the space, our specialist team talks it through with you and arranges a replacement or refund.

Do not cut corners on what ends up hidden. Our bathroom stage order is strip back to brick, first fix plumbing, tiling, then second fix. Once the tiles are on, first fix pipework is hard to reach, so it needs to be right first time. Specialist oversight is also worth paying for. It is included in our quotes, while some competitors subcontract the work without it.

## How our quotes work

Every customer gets an itemised quote with a detailed labour breakdown. You can see what each stage costs rather than a single lump sum.

Building material invoices and receipts are passed to you as they come in. We photograph and upload them, so you see the real cost of materials and there are no hidden costs. If something changes during the project, we amend it on the quote as it happens, so the final bill matches what you agreed.

Your quote includes waste removal, snagging and building materials. These are the items that often appear as extras on other quotes, so check for them when you compare.

## Getting an accurate quote

The more detail you give us at the start, the closer the quote will be to the final figure. Before we visit, it helps to have:

- Rough room measurements and photos of the current bathroom
- The fixtures you have bought or shortlisted, with model names
- Any layout changes you want, such as moving the shower or toilet
- Notes on access, including parking and stairs
