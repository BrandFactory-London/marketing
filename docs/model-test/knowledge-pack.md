# Knowledge pack: Migss Interiors website

## Tier 1: BrandFactory rules (apply to every post, never broken)

1. Never use em dashes. Use commas, full stops, colons or restructure the sentence.
2. No rule of three: do not use lists of three adjectives or three parallel phrases for rhythm ("fast, friendly and reliable").
3. No generic writing: every paragraph must carry a specific fact, example, number or practical instruction. No filler, no throat-clearing introductions.
4. Plain British English (colour, metre, organise). Short sentences.
5. Banned phrases: "not just X, but Y" and any "it's not X, it's Y" construction; "elevate"; "look no further"; "in today's fast-paced world"; "seamless"; "transform your space"; "dream bathroom"; "game-changer".
6. Facts discipline: state only facts that appear in this pack or that are common, verifiable trade knowledge (for example, what a wet room is, how a shower tray works). Never invent Migss statistics, prices, years in business, review counts, guarantees, accreditations or project examples. Where a Migss-specific figure is needed but is not in this pack, write a placeholder in square brackets, for example [Migss bathroom price range].

## Tier 2: Client knowledge (Migss Interiors)

- Business: bathroom and kitchen renovation company serving London, with a core area of Chigwell and Loughton, plus Ilford, Essex and Epping Forest.
- Voice: speak to homeowners as "you" and to Migss as "we". Prefer practical guidance over sales language.
- Two ways customers work with Migss:
  - Fit only: the customer has already bought a bathroom or fixtures from a showroom, and Migss installs them and renovates the room around them. This is the typical route.
  - Full design, supply and build: Migss runs a consultation, visits a showroom with the customer, designs and specifies the bathroom, produces a final drawing, then fits it.
- Quoting: customers get an itemised quote with a detailed labour breakdown. Building material invoices and receipts are passed to the customer as they come, photographed and uploaded, so there are no hidden costs. Changes are amended on the quote as they happen. The quote includes waste removal, snagging and building materials. Specialist oversight is included (some competitors subcontract without it).
- Design fee: design and specification is a separate stage with a flat fee [Migss design fee], which is deducted from the full design and build invoice.
- Pre-site phase: usually one to two weeks. The logistics team schedules deliveries, removes existing furniture and strip-out waste, and handles structural removal such as a partition wall, so the site is clean and nothing is installed out of order.
- On site: bathrooms take two to six weeks depending on size, scope, whether the layout changes, and access. Kitchens start in the same range but usually top out around four weeks.
- Main causes of delay: changes to plans, delivery errors, mixed-up drawings and access issues.
- Bathroom stage order: strip back to brick, first fix plumbing, tiling, then second fix.
- Cost: bathroom and kitchen price bands exist but are not in this pack; use [Migss bathroom price range] or [Migss kitchen price range]. Bathrooms vary widely by specification and size. Kitchens start lower but can cost much more where an extension or wall changes for open plan are involved.
- Materials: Migss works with high-quality brands; materials are to the customer's taste.
- If a customer has bought a product that will not work in the space, the Migss specialist team discusses it with them and arranges a replacement or refund.
