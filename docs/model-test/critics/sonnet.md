# Critic report (sonnet)

Mapping: D2, D4, D5 are the wet room comparison posts. D1, D3, D6 are the cost guides.
Checked across all six: no em dashes or en dashes, no banned words (seamless, elevate, game-changer and so on), British spelling holds, all internal links come from the allowed list. Word counts (including headings and excerpt): D1 852, D2 2489, D3 784, D4 2441, D5 1880, D6 883. All are inside spec. D2 sits right at the 2,500 ceiling. Excerpts are all under 160 characters.

## D1
Verdict: FAIL
Scores (1 to 10): voice 6, usefulness 7, specificity 6
Issues:
- [rule of three] "A bigger room means more tiling, more materials and more labour hours." : Three parallel "more X" phrases used for rhythm.
- [rule of three] "we will go through your room, your plans and your budget" : Three parallel "your X" phrases in the CTA, used for rhythm.
- [facts discipline] "We will not quote a tighter figure before seeing the room. Any firm that does is guessing." : Invented Migss policy plus an unsupported swipe at competitors. Neither is in the pack.
- [facts discipline] "Some competitors subcontract the work without it, and that is where mistakes slip through." : The pack says only that some competitors subcontract without oversight. "Mistakes slip through" is an added claim.
- [generic / vague] "The second route includes a design stage, so the total differs." : Vague, and it sits oddly with the correct later point that the design fee is deducted from the invoice.
- [generic] "Both vary widely from home to home, so one number never fits every bathroom." : Filler. The "Typical price range" section carries almost no information beyond the placeholder and a lower/upper end sketch.
- [quality] "The [our projects](/our-projects) page shows finished rooms." : Clumsy link text ("The our projects page"). The placeholder is also repeated five times, so the price section reads thin.
Best line: "Rushing or skipping a stage hides problems behind finished tiles."

## Ranking

1. D2: the most useful and specific post (floor type, joist direction, electrical zones, silicone, grout, upstairs leak risk) and a full recommendation set. Its faults are two small triples and one invented "we usually suggest" claim, all quick to fix.

Cost guides, best first:
